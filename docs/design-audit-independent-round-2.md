# Independent design audit — round 2

## Verdict

The seven-category goal is **not met**. One verified material interaction defect remains: at 390 × 600 with 18 px text, expanded SQL results cover the open mobile navigation drawer and intercept its Settings button. This affects navigation, overlay consistency, pointer access and the narrow layout. The other targeted states examined below show substantial improvement. Scores use the fixed rubric in `docs/design-audit.md`: a 9 requires polished, consistent inspected states, accessible critical controls and no verified material issue in that category; it is not universal accessibility or device certification.

## Ranked verified blocker

1. **High — expanded results intercept the mobile navigation drawer.** At 390 × 600 and 18 px, create and run a SQL script, expand results, then open navigation. The body-portalled results panel is drawn over the lower drawer, including Settings. The Settings button's rectangle was `(12, 551, 210, 37)` and its center hit the `.sql-results.is-expanded` element, not the button. An actual pointer click at that center left the Settings dialog closed and the results portal open. See `/tmp/hyperpg-design-audit/independent-round-2/independent-drawer-overlap.png`, `geometry-and-drawer.json` and `actual-drawer-click.json`. The current source gives the portalled results `z-index: 20` and the mobile drawer `z-index: 8` (scrim 7), consistent with the observed interception. Ensure the drawer and its scrim take precedence over the results or collapse the results when opening navigation; retain the working dialog focus and portal behavior. Recheck pointer targets and keyboard focus in both expanded and normal modes after a fix.

No other material blocker was verified in this scoped pass. This finding is separate from the baseline `sql-source` inert behavior, which remains implemented and was confirmed here.
