# Independent design audit — round 4

## Verdict and scores

The original obscured-background forward-Tab defect is fixed in the tested normal and expanded SQL paths. **The seven-category goal is not met.** Two independently reproduced resize/lifecycle defects can leave a visible workspace inert, including after an application dialog closes. The new large-text drawer header also has a visible focus-outline collision. The fixed rubric in `docs/design-audit.md` defines 9 as polished and consistent across inspected states, accessible critical controls, and no verified material issue; it does not require exhaustive assistive-technology or device certification.

## Verified findings

1. **High — resizing while a drawer-originated dialog is open corrupts inert state.** At 390 × 600, open navigation and choose **Add a database connection**. The New connection dialog has focus, and the sidebar and main workspace are inert. Resize to 1280 × 900 while the dialog remains open. The drawer cleanup restores the main panel to `inert=false` even though the application dialog is still open; programmatic focus of the SQL editor succeeds outside the dialog. Press Escape to close the dialog: its cleanup restores the *previously saved* `inert=true` to the main panel. The editor cannot be focused programmatically and an actual center click does not focus it. The dialog's Tab handler was tested separately; the focus escape described here was a programmatic focus attempt, not a claim that ordinary Tab escaped. Evidence: `resize-observation.json`, `resize-interaction.json`, `nested-resize-dialog.png`, and `nested-resize-after.png`. The overlapping save/restore lifecycles in `App.tsx` around the drawer layout effect and dialog effect explain the observed inversion. Recheck other app dialogs and nested/resize orderings after repair.

2. **High — crossing the overlay breakpoint leaves an apparently normal workspace inert.** At 390 × 600, open navigation and resize to 600 × 600. CSS changes the drawer to a persistent sidebar at **561 px**, hides the scrim and navigation trigger, and displays the workspace normally. The drawer still has `role=dialog`, `aria-modal`, the focus trap and `is-mobile-open`; the main panel remains inert. Tab stays in the sidebar and attempting to focus the editor fails. Escape closes the mobile state and restores the workspace, but there is no visible overlay cue explaining the trap. The close button is still visible at 600 px after that cleanup; clicking it leaves the persistent sidebar visible and has no visible effect. The effect uses `(max-width: 760px)`, whereas the overlay CSS uses `(max-width: 560px)`. Evidence: `resize-600-open.png`, `resize-600-closed.png`, `resize-observation.json`, and `resize-interaction.json`. A direct resize to 1280 without a nested app dialog is covered by both passing permanent theme tests; that does not cover either failure above.

3. **Moderate — large-text drawer heading collides with the new focus outline.** In both Light and Dark at 390 × 600 / 18 px, the heading ends at x=149.47 and the 42 × 42 close button starts at x=149.47, with zero gap. Its 2 px outline and 2 px offset extend over the heading. The adjacent add button ends at x=237.47 while the sidebar ends at x=235. The control centers remain hit-testable, but the focused header is visibly crowded and the focus ring crosses the title. See `light-normal-drawer.png`, `dark-normal-drawer.png`, and the `header` measurements in `light-keyboard.json` and `dark-keyboard.json`. At the default font, both close controls measure 28 × 28 and the captured headers have clear spacing. At 1280 × 900 the close button is hidden and the heading-actions wrapper and add button both measure 28 × 28 at x=202, preserving alignment (`desktop-sidebar.png`, `companion.json`).

## Verified ordinary flows and carried evidence

- At 390 × 600 / 18 px in Light and Dark, independently ran a mocked SQL query and opened navigation by focusing **Open navigation** and pressing Enter from both normal and expanded results. Initial focus was on the visible, center-hit-testable **Close navigation panel** control; the expanded results portal was removed. Each entry path ran 24 forward and 24 reverse Tab presses. Focus stayed inside the drawer on center-hit-testable controls, including close, add, search, tree controls and Settings. Configured Ctrl+N and Ctrl+W caused no new or closed workspace tab while navigation was open. The SQL draft and result remained. See the per-theme keyboard JSON and normal/expanded captures.
- Activated Settings with Enter, exercised 12 forward and 12 reverse Tabs in the dialog, and closed with Escape; focus returned to the navigation trigger and main inertness cleared. Separately, an actual center-point mouse click opened Settings after expansion and its visible close button returned to the workspace. In both themes, drawer close button, Escape and actual scrim click restored the workspace and focus. After a real 17 px SQL-workspace scroll, expansion, navigation and scrim close, the draft persisted; re-expansion, Restore and Escape worked and cleared SQL source inertness. Settings close measured 40 × 40 and was hit-testable in both 18 px captures.
- At default font, Light and Dark drawer captures show the unbranded header with a visible close control; focus cycles, Escape and focus return worked. The desktop companion retained the SQL draft and expansion/restore. At 390 × 600, selected a table through the drawer; Edit connection trapped focus and returned it to its drawer opener on Escape, then closing the drawer restored the main panel. With a staged table edit, the Unsaved changes guard trapped focus and returned to the drawer on Escape; discard-and-continue opened New connection, which trapped focus and returned to the drawer before the drawer closed normally. These are non-resize paths.
- The prior independent Round 2 broad visual evidence remains the basis for unchanged four-theme desktop table, Structure, connection, short desktop, tablet, long-name and sampled contrast judgments; the prior report notes the loading long-name SQL capture and contrast sampling limits. Round 3's expanded result geometry and scrolled result evidence also remains relevant. Only `App.tsx` and `frontend/public/style.css` differ from Round 3's per-file source list; the remaining 30 files, including the ActionGroup files, have identical hashes. New captures confirm the changed drawer and desktop header rather than reasserting a complete broad visual pass.
