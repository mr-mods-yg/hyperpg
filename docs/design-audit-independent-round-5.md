## Findings and evidence

There are **no verified blockers to rank** in this scoped pass. In particular:

- **Breakpoint restoration:** from 390 × 600 / 18 px, opened the drawer and crossed to 561, 600, 760 and 1280 px in Light and Dark. At each width the mobile state and scrim disappeared, the close control was hidden, the main panel was no longer inert, focus was visible, and an actual editor click and keyboard typing worked. A separate check opened the drawer at exactly 560 px and crossed to 561 px. The close control and scrim were present at 560 and removed/hidden at 561. `light-resize.json`, `dark-resize.json`, and `boundary-560.json` record the observations.
- **Overlapping isolation and focus:** for each of the four widths and both themes, opened New connection from the mobile drawer, checked that the sidebar and workspace were inert and that a programmatic editor focus attempt failed, then resized while the modal remained open. Twelve forward and twelve reverse Tabs stayed inside the modal. This remained true after resizing back to 390 px before closing it. After Escape, focus returned to a visible add button when the persistent sidebar was visible, or to Open navigation when the original opener was hidden; the editor then accepted a real click and typing. The opposite order—close the application dialog while the drawer remains open, then close the drawer—was repeated three times per theme: the workspace stayed inert until the drawer closed, then became usable. The direct browser test of `acquireInert` additionally covered an originally inert element, duplicate inputs, duplicate release, reacquisition and reverse owner release; original values were restored only after the last release (`inert-ownership.json`).
- **Other dialog lifecycles:** opened Edit connection from the tree, resized to 760 px with the dialog open, checked modal Tab containment and blocked background focus, closed it and actually edited a grid cell. With a staged edit, opened the Unsaved changes guard from the drawer, resized to 600 px, chose Discard and continue to transition into New connection, resized back to mobile, and closed it. The main panel remained inert under each modal and became interactive afterward. A separate guard-cancel-then-drawer-close sequence retained the staged draft and restored grid interaction. Settings opened from mobile and from the persistent sidebar was resized across the breakpoint in both directions; close returned focus to a visible Settings or Open navigation control, respectively, and the SQL editor accepted a click and typing.
- **Header geometry:** at 390 × 600 / 18 px in both themes, the heading ended at x=149.47 and the focused close control began at x=216, a 66.53 px gap versus a 2 px outline and 2 px offset. Close and Add each measured 42 × 42 px; Add ended at x=304 inside the 320 px drawer, and the 70 px scrim began at x=320 and was hit-testable. At default font the gap was 52.69 px, the controls were 28 × 28 px, and the 155 px scrim started at the 235 px edge. At 320 × 600 / 18 px the drawer was 276 px wide, leaving a hit-testable 44 px scrim; both 42 px header controls were hit-testable and inside the drawer. At 1280 × 900 the close button was hidden and the add button and actions wrapper shared the same 28 × 28 px default bounds (42 × 42 px at 18 px), preserving desktop alignment. See `*-geometry.json`, `*-preferences.json`, `light-18-header.png`, `dark-18-header.png`, `light-default-header.png`, `dark-default-header.png`, `light-320-header.png`, `dark-320-header.png`, and `desktop-sidebar.png` in the evidence directory.
- **Ordinary and expanded SQL:** in both themes at 390 × 600 / 18 px, opened the drawer from normal and expanded results. Initial focus was the visible, center-hit-testable close control; the expanded portal collapsed. For each entry, 24 forward and 24 reverse Tabs remained on visible, hit-testable drawer controls. Configured Ctrl+N and Ctrl+W did not create or close workspace tabs. Settings worked by keyboard and by an actual center-point pointer click; its 40 × 40 px close was hit-testable and return focus was correct. Escape, visible close, and actual scrim click restored the workspace. After a real 17 px workspace scroll, expansion, drawer open and close, the SQL draft persisted; re-expansion, Restore and Escape worked and cleared source inertness. Details and screenshots are in `light-keyboard.json`, `dark-keyboard.json`, and `*-normal/expanded-{drawer,settings}.png`.
- **Preferences and carried evidence:** the current sidebar is unbranded. Rendered Light SQL keyword/string samples were RGB 48/48/48 and 82/82/82; Dark samples were 238/238/238 and 194/194/194. Current tree and shared-button measurements are in `tree-button-metrics.json`; the Settings close measured 40 × 40 px in both themes. The previous independent four-theme table, Structure, sampled contrast, typography, long-name, short-desktop and tablet evidence remains relevant where the source is unchanged; it is described in `docs/design-audit-independent-round-2.md` and the round-four report. The earlier long-name SQL screenshot showed loading, and contrast samples were not exhaustive. Current desktop and mobile captures specifically inspect the changed sidebar rather than claiming a complete new broad visual pass.
